## Target users and working assumptions

Member-company technical leaders seek complementary capabilities and practical opportunities. Science-park innovation staff facilitate discovery and validate introductions. Assumptions requiring interviews: discovery is sufficiently slow to matter; complementary matches are useful; public information captures actionable capability; firms will review their profiles.

## Interview questions

1. Describe the last time you searched for a research or company partner.
2. Which sources and people did you use, and where did you get stuck?
3. How do you decide whether a capability claim is credible?
4. Which proposal would be worth a first conversation, and why?
5. What information must remain private?
6. Would you update a public profile? What would make that worthwhile?
7. Who could approve a pilot, and what outcome would justify continuing?
